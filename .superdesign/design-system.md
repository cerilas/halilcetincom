# Halil Çetin Hair Transplant — Design system

## Product
Premium İstanbul saç ekimi kliniği. Hedef: doğal saç çizgisi, şeffaf süreç, ücretsiz analiz dönüşümü. Dil: Türkçe. SEO ve admin CMS ayrı katmanlarda kalır; bu dosya yalnızca görsel dili tanımlar.

## Brand
- Name: Halil Çetin / Halil Çetin Hair Transplant
- Mark: 6px gold disc + tracked uppercase wordmark in a glass pill. No invented logo, initials, or SVG mark.
- Voice: clinical, precise, unshowy luxury.

## Color
- Background `#07080b`
- Foreground `#f3eee4`
- Gold `#c4a46a`
- Gold soft `#e8d5a3`
- Muted `#9a9386`
- Line `rgba(243,238,228,0.10)`
- Card `#101216`
Do not introduce purple, neon, pink, or unrelated gradients.

## Type
- Body / UI: Geist Sans
- Display: Instrument Serif, italic allowed on the last headline clause
- Eyebrows: 11–12px, tracking 0.28–0.32em, gold, uppercase
- H1: 2.6rem → 6–8xl, leading ~0.95
- Buttons: 12–14px, tracking wide, pill

## Layout
- Max width 72rem (`max-w-6xl`)
- Horizontal padding 1.25rem
- Cards `rounded-2xl`, CTAs `rounded-full`
- Fixed glass header (logo left, pill nav center, gold Analiz right)
- Gold 2px scroll progress on the top edge

## Motion
- Spotlight fade-in
- Word-stagger headline
- Magnetic pill buttons
- 3D card tilt
- Infinite marquee
- Scroll-scrub process rail
- Respect reduced motion

## Components
- Header / Footer as shared shell
- PinCard for treatments
- NumberTicker for stats
- FAQ accordion with +/–
- Border-beam CTA band
